How the arrival time is worked out

Countdown is the time left until the next departure in the timetable, using Singapore time.

Door to door adds your walk to the pickup point, the wait for the shuttle, and the ride. The wait is whatever is left after you have walked there.

Arrival is the departure time plus the ride time.

If the shuttle leaves sooner than your walk takes, it is flagged as a likely miss and the next one is used for the breakdown.
